隐私政策

最后更新：2026-10-25 · 适用于 vansiqi.com
TL;DR：对本站访客，我们不使用跟踪 Cookie，也不收集任何个人数据。如果你订阅公司变动 提醒，我们只要求最基本的信息——你的邮箱——并且只用它发送你订阅的提醒。我们承诺绝不出售 你的数据：从来没有，将来也不会。

Data controller

VanSiqi — an independent republisher of public company-registry data, run by a small independent team. We are not a government agency and not affiliated with any registry.
Contact: privacy@vansiqi.com (activated at launch domain is live)

数据控制者

梵思琦（VanSiqi）——官方公司登记数据的独立再发布者，由一个小的独立团队维护。我们不是 政府机构，与任何登记处均无隶属关系。
联系方式：privacy@vansiqi.com （上线时启用）

Two types of data we handle

  • Visitor data (you browsing this site): we track no individual people. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics for aggregate traffic numbers only — no cookies, no fingerprints, no personal data.
  • Subscriber data (you as a subscriber): your email address and the list of companies you asked us to watch (up to 5 on the free tier). That's the whole list.

我们处理两类数据

  • 访客数据（浏览本站的你）：我们不追踪任何个人。流量统计只使用 Cloudflare Web Analytics 的聚合数据——无 Cookie、无指纹、无个人数据。
  • 订阅者数据（订阅提醒的你）：你的邮箱地址，以及你要求监控的公司清单（免费档最多 5 家）。清单就这些。

As a visitor to this site

  • No personal data about visitors is collected or stored
  • No information such as cookies is stored in your browser
  • No information about visitors is sold or shared with advertising companies
  • Aggregate traffic stats come from Cloudflare Web Analytics, which by their own documentation does not collect or use visitors' personal data

作为本站访客

  • 不收集、不存储任何访客个人数据
  • 不在你的浏览器中存储 Cookie 等信息
  • 不出售访客信息，不与广告公司共享，不用于广告目的
  • 聚合流量统计来自 Cloudflare Web Analytics——按其官方文档，它不收集、不使用访客个人数据

As a subscriber

Our guiding principle: collect only what we need, and use it only to provide the service you signed up for. An email address is required to subscribe — that's so we can send you the alerts and so you can unsubscribe from them later. There is nothing else to sign up for, and no other purpose for your address. Subscribing is voluntary: everything on this site except the email alerts works without any personal data.

作为订阅者

我们的原则：只收集必需的，且只用于提供你订阅的服务。订阅需要一个邮箱——用于给你 发提醒，也让你以后能退订。除此之外没有别的注册项，你的邮箱也没有其他用途。订阅完全自愿： 除了邮件提醒，本站的一切功能均无需任何个人信息。

Legal basis for processing (GDPR / PIPL)

We process your email under your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); PIPL Art. 13), which you can withdraw at any time with effect for the future.

处理的法律依据（GDPR / PIPL）

我们依据你的同意处理你的邮箱（《个人信息保护法》第 13 条；GDPR 第 6 条 第 1 款 a 项），你可随时撤回，撤回仅对未来生效。

Subprocessors

  • Cloudflare (US): website hosting and traffic analytics
  • Email delivery provider (to be announced): sending your change-alert emails. When we pick one, we will list it here — before the first alert is ever sent.

These are the only services that touch subscriber data.

子处理商

  • Cloudflare（美国）：网站托管与流量统计
  • 邮件发送服务商（待定）：发送变动提醒邮件。选定后我们会在此列出——会在发出第一封 提醒邮件之前。

只有这些服务会接触到订阅者数据。

International data transfers

Your email is stored on servers outside China, and our processors operate outside the EU/UK as well. For subscribers in China: under PIPL Art. 39 we separately disclose the overseas recipients, purpose, data categories and how to exercise your rights — that is what the extra checkbox on the signup form is for. The volume falls under the exemption in China's 2024 cross-border provisions (Art. 5), but we still ask for your separate consent. For EU/UK visitors: the transfer is based on your explicit consent (GDPR Art. 49(1)(a)); ask us for a copy of whatever gets transferred.

跨境数据传输

你的邮箱存储于中国境外的服务器，我们的服务商也在欧盟/英国之外运营。对中国的订阅者： 依据《个人信息保护法》第 39 条，我们单独告知境外接收方、处理目的、数据种类及权利行使方式—— 订阅表单中那个额外的勾选框就是为了这件事。数据量适用中国 2024 年跨境新规第 5 条的豁免 （年出境不满 10 万人、非敏感信息），但我们仍然单独征求你的同意。对欧盟/英国访客：传输基于你的 明确同意（GDPR 第 49 条第 1 款 a 项），需要被传输数据的副本请向我们索取。

Retention of data

We keep your email for as long as your subscription is active. Unconfirmed sign-ups are deleted automatically after 14 days. When you unsubscribe, your address comes off the sending list right away — we keep only the address and the date, for 24 months, so we don't email you again by accident. Want it erased sooner? Ask, and we'll filter you out manually.

数据保留

订阅有效期内保留你的邮箱。未确认的申请 14 天后自动删除。退订后，你的邮箱立刻 移出发送名单——我们只留邮箱和日期，24 个月，防止误发。想提前删掉？说一声，我们改成人工过滤。

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time (one click, immediate)
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Lodge a complaint with your data-protection authority — UK: the ICO; China: the CAC or your local internet authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@vansiqi.com or use the unsubscribe link in any alert email. We reply within 14 days.

你的权利

你有权：

  • 访问你的个人数据
  • 更正不准确的数据
  • 请求删除你的数据
  • 随时撤回同意（一次点击，立即生效）
  • 限制或拒绝处理
  • 向你的数据保护机构投诉——英国：ICO； 中国：国家网信办或属地网信部门

行使方式：联系 privacy@vansiqi.com，或点击任意提醒邮件中的退订 链接。我们 14 天内回复。

Data licensing

The registry records republished on this site come from official registries under their own open-data licences (for UK data: the Open Government Licence v3.0 — "Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0."). Licensing terms for other registries are listed on our About page. This privacy policy covers your email address; the company records themselves are public data.

数据许可

本站转载的登记记录来自各官方登记处，适用其各自的开放数据许可（英国数据适用 Open Government Licence v3.0——「Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.」）。其他登记处的许可条款见关于页。本隐私政策 管的是你的邮箱；公司记录本身是公开数据。

Changes and questions

We may update this policy to comply with regulations or reflect new practices. Whenever we make a significant change, we will update the date above and tell subscribers in the alert email itself — before the change takes effect.
